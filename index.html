StairSync

A personal Android app for StairMaster workouts.

StairSync builds a new ~45-minute Spotify playlist each day to fit that day's StairMaster workout: intervals, pyramids, climbs, flat roads, or letting the music set the pace. During the workout it beeps when it's time to go up or down a level.

Songs are matched to the workout by tempo. Tempo (BPM) data comes from GetSongBPM and Deezer.

StairSync is a private, non-commercial project for personal use.

BPM data provided by GetSongBPM.com.
